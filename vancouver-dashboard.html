🛡️ Crime data coverage

These incident markers come from the Vancouver Police Department and cover only the city of Vancouver — the area inside the dashed boundary on the map.

The rest of Clark County (unincorporated areas, Camas, Battle Ground, Washougal, Ridgefield, La Center) is policed by the Clark County Sheriff's Office and other municipal departments. None currently publish an open, mappable incident feed, so no dots there means no public data — not necessarily no crime.

We're tracking options for county-wide coverage. This map will be the first place it shows up if that changes.

About this map

This map puts public data about Vancouver, WA on one screen: crime, collisions, property values, construction, parks, transit, rail, restaurants, culture and more.

Turn layers on and off from the four buttons in the top bar. Some detailed layers (property values, restaurants, apartments) ask you to zoom in first — this keeps the map fast.

Click any dot, line, or shape on the map for details.

Data sources: City of Vancouver GIS (Vancouver Police Dept. crime), Clark County GIS (parcels, collisions, parks, rail, transit, construction), National Weather Service, OpenStreetMap contributors.

Data belongs to its respective public agencies and may lag real time. This tool is an independent community project, not an official city product.
